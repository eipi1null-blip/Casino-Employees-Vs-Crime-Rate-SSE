Key idea: The least-squares regression line is the line of fit that minimizes the sum of the squared residuals.

What you are seeing

Residuals measure vertical error.

A residual is the observed crime rate minus the rate predicted by the line. Blue bars are positive residuals; coral bars are negative.

SSE = Σ(y − ŷ)²

Squaring prevents positive and negative errors from canceling. “Least squares” means choosing the line with the least possible total of these squared errors.
